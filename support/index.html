Blackkeep

A dark roguelike deckbuilder for iPhone. You command the monsters; the crusade is coming.

Need help? Email support.blackkeep@gmail.com.

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version (iPhone Settings > General > About) and what happened just before the problem. A screenshot helps.

Purchases

I bought something but it isn't showing.

Open the game's Store or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, and that you're online.

I got a new iPhone. Do I have to buy again?

No. Install Blackkeep on the new iPhone and tap Restore Purchases. Everything you bought comes back.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and choose the purchase.

Is anything random for money?

No. Every purchase is a fixed item: the Blood Pact, or a coven with a listed set of cards. There are no loot boxes and nothing you buy runs out.

Your progress

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, after every move. There's no account. Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup includes it, so restoring a backup brings it back.

Will deleting the app delete my progress?

Yes. Deleting Blackkeep deletes its saved progress from that iPhone. Purchases are safe: Restore Purchases brings them back.

Ads

Do I have to watch ads?

Never. Ads are only offered for an optional bonus, and every offer can be skipped. The Blood Pact gives those bonuses without the ads.

The game asked about tracking. What should I choose?

Either answer is fine. The game and its rewards are the same whatever you choose. You can change it later in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

I saw an ad that shouldn't be there.

In the game, open Settings and tap Report an ad, or email us. Tell us what the ad showed and when you saw it.

Where are my ad privacy choices?

Settings > Privacy choices in the game (shown where the law requires it).

Game Center and the Daily Dread

How do I get on the leaderboards?

Sign in to Game Center in iPhone Settings > Game Center. The Long Night and the Daily Dread each have a leaderboard. Without Game Center the game plays normally, just without leaderboards.

When does the Daily Dread change?

Every day at midnight UTC. Everyone plays the same run that day, and you get one try.

Other questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, the game plays offline. The Store, ads and leaderboards need a connection.

Crash reports

They're off unless you agree when the game asks. You can change it any time in the game's Settings > Send crash reports.
